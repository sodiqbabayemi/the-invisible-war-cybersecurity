## Learning Objectives

By engaging with *The Invisible War – Volume I: The Invisible Attacker*, learners will be able to:

1. **Understand the foundations of offensive cybersecurity** and the role of ethical hacking in identifying and strengthening security weaknesses.

2. **Apply responsible and ethical security practices** by conducting cybersecurity activities only within authorized and controlled environments.

3. **Understand common offensive cybersecurity concepts, tools, and techniques** used during security assessment and penetration-testing activities.

4. **Build and operate safe cybersecurity practice environments** using tools such as Kali Linux, VirtualBox, and intentionally vulnerable applications.

5. **Perform controlled hands-on security exercises** using platforms such as OWASP Juice Shop to develop practical offensive-security skills.

6. **Develop a structured cybersecurity methodology** for approaching reconnaissance, analysis, vulnerability identification, exploitation in controlled environments, and security improvement.

7. **Recognize the importance of authorization, responsible disclosure, and rules of engagement** when performing cybersecurity testing.

8. **Connect cybersecurity theory with practical laboratory experience**, transforming concepts into repeatable technical skills.

9. **Develop a security-focused mindset** that emphasizes understanding vulnerabilities so they can be identified, mitigated, and ultimately strengthened.

10. **Progress toward practical offensive cybersecurity competency** through structured exercises, controlled experimentation, and continuous learning.
